Programs

The American Patriot Family Foundation supports initiatives that strengthen communities and recognize service across the United States. Through its program areas, the Foundation partners with organizations and community leaders working to improve lives and expand opportunity for individuals and families.

Literacy

The Foundation supports literacy initiatives designed to expand access to reading and learning opportunities for individuals across the country. These programs promote early literacy, improve reading proficiency, and provide resources for lifelong learning, particularly for those who demonstrate curiosity, dedication, and a desire to grow. Through literacy support, the Foundation seeks to empower individuals with the knowledge and skills needed to participate fully and meaningfully in society.
Literacy

Medical Research

The Foundation supports medical research focused on advancing treatment, early detection, and patient care. Through charitable contributions and partnerships with leading research institutions and nonprofit organizations, these efforts help accelerate discovery and expand access to care for individuals and families facing serious illness.
Medical Research

Continued Education

The Foundation supports continued education initiatives that expand access to learning at every stage of life. These programs promote literacy, historical understanding, and lifelong learning — including the Adventures of Rush Revere series, which brings American history to life for middle grade readers in classrooms and homes across the country.

Foster Care Advocacy

The Foundation supports organizations working to improve the lives of children and families involved in foster care and adoption systems. These initiatives focus on programs that provide stability, mentorship, and long-term support for children in need of safe and supportive homes. The Foundation believes that strengthening families and supporting vulnerable children is an essential part of building stronger communities.
