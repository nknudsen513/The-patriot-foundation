Literacy

At the Military Kids Scholarship Foundation, we believe the story of a military child is one of resilience, character, and quiet strength. From relocations and reintegration to long seasons of separation, these experiences shape young leaders in extraordinary ways.
Our scholarships are designed to invest in students who have grown up in service families and are ready to pursue their educational goals with purpose and determination.
Below you’ll find three of our current scholarship opportunities and instructions for applying.

BRAVE VOICES SCHOLARSHIP

For military-connected students who share their story
The Brave Voices Scholarship recognizes students who reflect thoughtfully on how life in a military family has shaped their identity and aspirations. Applicants are invited to submit a 750–1,000 word essay describing a defining moment connected to service and how it influenced their future goals.
Apply Now
Submit your story here. Awards are granted based on clarity of voice, authenticity, and demonstrated perseverance.

DEPLOYMENT RESILIENCE GRANT

Supporting academic strength through transition
This grant is available to students whose families have experienced one or more deployments during their academic journey. The award honors those who have maintained focus, discipline, and growth despite change or uncertainty.
Apply Now
Applicants should provide a short personal statement along with one academic recommendation.

SERVICE LEGACY SCHOLARSHIP

Honoring commitment across generations
The Service Legacy Scholarship supports students whose parent or guardian has served—or currently serves—in the Armed Forces. Applicants are encouraged to share how their family’s service has influenced their values, leadership, and educational plans.
Apply Now
Eligible students must submit verification of service and a personal narrative outlining their academic objectives.
We look forward to reviewing each submission and celebrating the accomplishments of military-connected scholars.
Application deadlines and additional details are listed within each form link above.
