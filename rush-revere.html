About the Series

America is turning 250 – the perfect time for middle grade students to Rush, Rush, Rush into history!

The Rush Revere series is a five-book middle grade series that lets young readers travel back in time and experience American history firsthand. Join American history teacher Rush Revere, his hilarious sidekick Liberty the talking Horse, and the Manchester Middle School students as they meet famous figures, visit important places, and see history happen right in front of them.

Written by Rush and Kathryn Adams Limbaugh, this #1 New York Times bestselling and Children’s Choice Award-winning series brings America’s founding story to life for young readers.

Summary

For America’s 250th anniversary, students don’t just read about history – they jump right into it with the Crew.

Through time travel, readers join Rush Revere, Liberty the horse, and the Manchester Middle School students as they go directly into America’s founding story. They visit Holland, the Mayflower, Plymouth Colony, colonial Boston, Revolutionary War sites, Washington, D.C., Fort McHenry, and the early presidency. Along the way, they meet historical figures including William Bradford, Squanto, Samuel Adams, Paul Revere, George Washington, Abigail Adams, Thomas Jefferson, James Madison, Dolley Madison, and Francis Scott Key.

It’s American history up close – exciting, memorable, and fun for middle grade readers.
