Rush Revere

The Adventures of Rush Revere is the #1 New York Times bestselling series by Rush and Kathryn Limbaugh, bringing American history to life through time travel, adventure, humor, and unforgettable characters.

Summary of the Series

The Adventures of Rush Revere is the #1 New York Times bestselling American history series created by Rush Limbaugh and Kathryn Adams Limbaugh. Blending humor, adventure, and unforgettable characters with the remarkable story of America’s founding, the series brings history to life for young readers and families.
Join Rush Revere, his time-traveling talking horse Liberty, and their friends as they race through American history, meeting exceptional Americans and experiencing defining moments firsthand. From the Pilgrims and the American Revolution to the courage, sacrifice, and ideas that shaped the nation, each adventure makes learning history exciting, accessible, and fun.
With millions of books in print and multiple #1 New York Times bestsellers, The Adventures of Rush Revere has introduced a generation of readers to the people and principles behind the American story. Now the adventure continues—inviting families everywhere to discover history, celebrate America, and have a lot of fun along the way.

In Classrooms Everywhere

Teachers, parents and young readers across the country have made Rush Revere part of how a generation meets American history.

The Five-Book Series

1

Rush Revere and the Brave Pilgrims

Travel aboard the Mayflower and experience Plymouth, the first Thanksgiving, and the courage and perseverance that shaped early America.

2

Rush Revere and the First Patriots

Meet Benjamin Franklin and other remarkable patriots as Rush Revere explores the ideas, ingenuity, and determination behind America’s fight for freedom.

3

Rush Revere and the American Revolution

Experience the American Revolution firsthand as Rush Revere meets George Washington and discovers the courage, sacrifice, and leadership that won independence.

4

Rush Revere and the Star-Spangled Banner

Journey through American history to discover the Constitution, national symbols, and inspiring stories behind the freedoms Americans continue to cherish today.

5

Rush Revere and the Presidency

Meet America’s early presidents and explore the challenges, responsibilities, and character required to lead a new nation and protect its ideals.
