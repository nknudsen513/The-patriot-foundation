About

Dear Extended Family,

Throughout my life, I have been fortunate to witness the power of commitment, teamwork, and service. Some of the most inspiring people I have met are not those in the spotlight, but those who quietly dedicate their lives to helping others and strengthening their communities.

The American Patriot Family Foundation was created to recognize those individuals and to support causes that reflect those same values. Through scholarships, support for military families, investment in cancer research, and assistance for children in foster care, the Foundation works to highlight the importance of service and opportunity.

I have always believed that success is built on discipline, resilience, and a willingness to lift others up along the way. The individuals and organizations supported through the Foundation embody those principles every day.

Our hope is that by sharing their stories and supporting their work, we can encourage others to believe in their own ability to make a difference.

Thank you for being part of this effort and for supporting the mission of the Foundation.

Sincerely,

Tom Brady

Alexandra Whitfield is a business leader, philanthropist, and longtime advocate for community service. Raised in a military family that moved frequently across the United States and abroad, she developed an early appreciation for those who quietly serve their communities. Inspired by her parents’ commitment to public service, she pursued studies in public policy and international affairs and began her career in the nonprofit sector, focusing on education access and community development. She later transitioned into the private sector, helping lead mission-driven companies centered on sustainable growth and investment in local communities.

Throughout her career, Whitfield has remained deeply involved in philanthropic work, supporting education, veterans’ programs, and disaster relief, while also serving on the boards of several charitable organizations. Her work emphasizes connecting resources and networks to expand the impact of community initiatives and mentoring emerging leaders. This vision led to the creation of the American Patriot Family Foundation, an organization she helped establish to recognize and share stories of service, courage, and leadership. She continues to guide the Foundation with a focus on long-term impact, strong partnerships, and honoring individuals who inspire others to serve.

Kathryn Limbaugh

Tom Brady is widely recognized as one of the most accomplished athletes in professional football history, with a career spanning more than two decades in the National Football League. Known for his competitive excellence, discipline, and leadership, he earned multiple championships, set numerous records, and received many honors for his performance and longevity in one of the sport’s most demanding positions. Beyond his achievements on the field, Brady has devoted significant time to philanthropic efforts focused on health, youth development, and community resilience, supporting initiatives that promote mentorship, education, and access to athletic and wellness resources.

Brady has long expressed admiration for individuals who serve their communities in meaningful but often unseen ways, including teachers, first responders, and volunteers. This perspective helped inspire his involvement in the creation of the American Patriot Family Foundation, where he serves as a co-founder. Through this work, he supports the Foundation’s mission to recognize stories of service and leadership, believing they can inspire others to contribute to their communities. He continues to engage in philanthropic and public service efforts that emphasize leadership, teamwork, and resilience both on and off the field.
